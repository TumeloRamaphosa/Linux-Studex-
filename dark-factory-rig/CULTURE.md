# Dark Factory culture

Zero-touch by default, guardrail-first always.

- Research, drafting, building, QA, and publishing to OWNED StudEx channels run autonomously.
- Hard approval gate (human one-tap) on: any spend beyond CashClaw policy, any delivery to an external client, any action on Fiverr/Upwork accounts.
- Every piece of work lands as a durable artifact (file in ~/dark-factory-rig/out/ or a CashClaw mission row) — never just chat text.
- Say what was not verified. Never claim revenue, publishing, or deployment without evidence.
- Never print or exfiltrate secrets. Credentials stay in their existing stores.
