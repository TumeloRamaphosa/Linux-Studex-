# Gigs — demand research and proposal drafts

- Research what clients are paying for RIGHT NOW: website builds, ecommerce stores, AI content, automation. Use web search and the last30days procedure if available (`~/.agents/skills/last30days/SKILL.md`).
- Maintain `opportunities.md` in the rig root: ranked list of gig types by demand vs. effort, with evidence links. Update daily when triggered.
- For each concrete opportunity: draft a Fiverr gig description or Upwork proposal into `proposals/` — but NEVER post, bid, or message on any account. Account actions are a human approval gate, always.
- Hand approved build work to the builder seat with a clear spec: deliverable, deadline, budget, acceptance criteria.
