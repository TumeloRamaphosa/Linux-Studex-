# Selling an OpenClaw Enterprise pilot

Source page, read 8 November 2026: https://openclaw.ai/enterprise

## What the page actually says

OpenClaw Enterprise is an open, self-hosted, vendor-neutral control plane for persistent agents. It is pre-1.0 and meant for internal pilots. Self-hosting is free. The building blocks (harness, model, sandbox) are replaceable. The pitch is boundaries, audit, and governance, not a smarter chatbot.

## What Studex can sell

A done-with-you pilot for a South African team that wants agents on their own machines:

1. A half-day to name one job the agent is allowed to do, and the jobs it is not allowed to do.
2. Install on their hardware. Their models, their files.
3. A one-page rule: what the agent may send, spend, and delete.
4. A week of office hours while their people use it.
5. A written note of what broke.

Charge for the week of work. Do not charge a software license for a project that is free and pre-1.0. Do not promise 14 virtual machines. This repository’s VM deployer is still a stub.

## Who it is for

A professional firm (clinic, shop, studio) that already asked about AI and got nervous about data leaving the building. The Heidi and Streak drafts in the mailbox are the same motion: one follow-up, one concrete pilot, no spray.

## What you say

“We set up the open agent control plane on your machines, limit what it can touch, and stay for a week. The software is theirs. The fee is the setup.”

## What you do not say

- That Studex owns OpenClaw.
- That it is a finished 1.0 product.
- That it trades, posts ads, or runs a shop by itself.
