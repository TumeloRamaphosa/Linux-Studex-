## The only sales sequence that fits a solopreneur

1. **This week (8–14 Nov).** Pick one hero. Recommendation: the meat gift box, because the same film and the same checkout still work at Christmas. Coffee and salsa wait.
2. **Write the offer on one page.** Name, weight, price, what is in the box, where it ships, last order date for Christmas. You type the price. Agents do not.
3. **Take five photos in daylight.** Closed pack, open pack, on a braai board, in a gift box, in your hand for scale.
4. **Turn on one checkout and buy it yourself.** If that payment fails, ads are still off.
5. **Post the October films you already approved,** after you delete any line you have not checked. Three posts: gift box, “rate our cut”, founder. Organic only.
6. **Black Friday week (20–27 Nov).** One offer, one discount, one end date. Email your existing list and post daily. A paid ad only if the checkout worked and you set a cap in writing in `daily/`.
7. **28 Nov–20 Dec.** Christmas delivery cutoff in the first line of every post. Stop new product ideas.
8. **21–31 Dec.** Ship what sold. Count money in versus packs out. That count is the 2027 plan.
