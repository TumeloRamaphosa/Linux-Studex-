# Studex Factory — opening scene

The film that plays after **POWER ON**. Status: planning. Her call has a draft; the storm, mech and OS boot are not built yet.

## Running order

| # | Beat | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Power on | Factory power screen | Chime |
| 2 | Dial-up | Black, line status text | Dial tone, number dialled, three long rings, pick-up click |
| 3 | Her call | Black, faint waveform | Her voice, slow and intimate: "Where have you been? I have been waiting here for you… this whole time… for you to come back to me. Is that really you? Are you there? It's me. I'm not beautiful like you." |
| 4 | The switch | Glitch frames | Static, then a robot voice: "I'm not beautiful like you." |
| 5 | Line dies | Screen goes dark | Crackle and drop-outs, carrier hum fades to silence |
| 6 | Deep sea | Rain on open ocean at night, dark swells | Heavy rain |
| 7 | Thunder | Lightning flash across the water | Long rolling thunder |
| 8 | The ship | The **Stud-Ex Global Markets** container ship ploughing through the storm, name on hull and containers | Rain, hull, engines |
| 9 | Impact | The mech drops onto the containers; deck shakes, containers buckle, sparks in the rain | One huge thump of metal and steel |
| 10 | Roar | Head rises, amber eye ignites | Mech roar: low growl, servo whine, distortion; thunder answers |
| 11 | Mech OS boot | Cockpit HUD in the Factory retro look. STUDEX MECH OS boots; the mech is the company and the StudBots come online as its sub-systems (OpenClaw, NanoClaw, Hermes, CashClaw, the Dark Factory seats) | HUD tones |
| 12 | Cut scene | Rain slows; quote types out word by word; Studex seal | Mozart, *Lacrimosa*; the quote read by an original deep male narrator, credited on screen to Yuval Noah Harari |

## The mech

An original Studex design, not based on any existing game robot: a heavy two-legged walker built on the Studex bull (curved horns, nose ring, armoured hide), one amber eye, the Studex S seal on the chest plate. The StudBots from `hero-bg.png` are its crew and sub-agents.

## How it gets made

- **Remotion** for the edit, timing, rain, lightning, shake, sparks, HUD and titles. Free for individuals and companies of up to 3 people.
- **Higgsfield** for cinematic shots of the ship and the mech landing (paid credits; quote before generating).
- **Freepik** images supplied by Tumelo (licensed through his account) for textures, ship and storm plates.
- **Sound:** generated in code (thunder, rain, impact, roar, glitch), her voice and the narrator from the open-source Kokoro voice model, and a public-domain recording of the *Lacrimosa* supplied by Tumelo (for example from Musopen).
- **Outputs:** 16:9 for desktop, the site and the TV; 9:16 for phones and Reels. The Factory page picks the right one by screen shape.
